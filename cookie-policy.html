Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on the Nine3 Designs website.

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar technologies that can be stored on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit a website.

They can be used to help websites function correctly, remember preferences, improve performance and, where applicable, understand how visitors use a website.

2. How Nine3 Designs Uses Cookies

Nine3 Designs aims to keep its website simple and privacy-conscious.

We may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies where they are essential for the website to operate, for example where required for basic functionality, security or technical operation.

Where cookies or similar technologies are not strictly necessary, they will only be used where the appropriate consent has been obtained.

3. Analytics And Optional Cookies

Nine3 Designs does not intend to use non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technologies without the appropriate consent.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, social media tracking or other non-essential technologies in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated to explain what technologies are being used, why they are used and how you can manage your preferences.

4. Third-Party Services

Some third-party services used to support the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies. These services may include external website features, embedded content, hosting, forms or other technical services.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy and cookie policies, and their use of information is governed by their own terms and policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to choose whether cookies are accepted and whether existing cookies should be deleted.

Please be aware that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the way some websites or features operate.

6. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, particularly if the technologies used on the website change or if relevant legal or regulatory requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
