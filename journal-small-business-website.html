Five things every small business website needs

9 September 2026 · 4 min read · by Natalie

Before anyone argues about colours or fonts, a small business website has five jobs to do. Get these right and the pretty bits have something to stand on. Miss them and no amount of design will save it.

1. Say what you do in the first five seconds

A visitor should answer three questions instantly: what do you do, who is it for, and what should I do next? Not a clever slogan — a plain statement. "Beautiful websites for South Yorkshire businesses. Free quote." beats "Designing tomorrow's dreams today" every single time.

2. Make it easy to contact you

Your phone number or email should never be more than one click away, on every page, on a mobile especially. If someone has to hunt for how to reach you, they will reach one of your competitors instead.

3. Show proof

Real reviews, real project photos, real names. Three genuine testimonials beat a wall of marketing claims. If you have happy customers, put them on the site — word for word, name and all.

4. Work properly on a phone

Most of your visitors are on a phone, holding it with one hand, possibly walking. Buttons big enough to tap, text big enough to read, pages light enough to load on a dodgy signal. This is not a "nice to have" any more.

5. One clear next step

Every page should end somewhere: get a quote, book a call, view the work. One obvious button, repeated where it makes sense. A website without a next step is a brochure; a website with one is a salesman.

A good small business website is not clever. It is clear, quick, honest and easy to contact — then the design makes it memorable.
