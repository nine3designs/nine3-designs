The Finer Details

Papers, Foils &
Envelopes.

Everything that turns a beautiful design into a beautiful object: paper choices, printing finishes, envelope colours and seals.

01 · Paper & Card

It starts with
the paper.

Every suite begins with the paper you hold. Papers are typically 300–450gsm, substantial, card-like pieces that arrive feeling special. I'll order samples so you can feel the difference before you decide.

Smooth Uncoated

  • Crisp, modern and easy to write on
  • A favourite for invitations and RSVP cards
  • Available in a range of weights

Textured & Felted

  • Soft, tactile finish with a gentle texture
  • Romantic, slightly vintage feel
  • Beautiful paired with foil detailing

Kraft & Recycled

  • Warm, natural tone with an honest feel
  • Suits relaxed and outdoor weddings
  • Recycled options available
02 · Foiling & Finishes

Details that
catch the light.

Finishes can be added to almost any suite. Choose one, or combine a few.

Foiling

  • Gold, rose gold, silver and copper
  • Black or white foil on darker papers
  • Holographic for a modern twist

Embossing & Debossing

  • Raised or pressed detailing
  • Elegant without any ink at all
  • Lovely for monograms and motifs

The Little Extras

  • Spot UV highlights
  • Painted edges
  • Rounded corners & perforations

Before You Approve

A Fresh Pair
Of Eyes Helps.

Before your artwork goes to print, please take the time to carefully check every detail.

Once approved, your stationery will be printed exactly as shown.

Please check:

  • ☆Spelling of all names
  • ☆Wedding date and ceremony time
  • ☆Venue names and addresses
  • ☆RSVP details and deadlines
  • ☆Contact information and website links
  • ☆Gift list information
  • ☆Guest names on personalised stationery
  • ☆Table numbers and seating arrangements
Top Tip

Ask a friend or family member to review your proofs too. A fresh pair of eyes can often spot something you've missed.
