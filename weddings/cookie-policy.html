Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on the Nine3 Weddings website.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites function correctly, remember preferences and, in some cases, understand how visitors use a website.

2. How Nine3 Weddings Uses Cookies

Nine3 Weddings aims to keep its website simple, functional and privacy-conscious.

We may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies where they are required for the website to operate correctly, maintain security or provide functionality you have requested.

3. Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to function properly. These may support basic website functionality, security or other essential features.

Because these technologies are necessary for the website to operate, they may not always require consent under applicable data protection rules.

4. Analytics and Optional Cookies

Nine3 Weddings may introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies in the future.

If we do so, this Cookie Policy will be updated to explain what technologies are being used, why they are used and how you can manage your preferences.

Non-essential cookies and similar technologies will only be used where the appropriate consent has been obtained.

5. Third-Party Services

Some features or services used by Nine3 Weddings may be provided by third-party companies. These services may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their functionality.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we recommend reviewing where relevant.

6. Contact Forms and External Services

If you use a contact form or other interactive feature on the Nine3 Weddings website, the service providing that functionality may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to operate the service, prevent abuse or maintain security.

These third-party services may process information in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

7. Managing Cookies

You can control, block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to choose whether to accept cookies, notify you when cookies are being used or delete cookies that have already been stored.

Please be aware that disabling necessary cookies may affect how some parts of the website function.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time if the technologies or services used on the Nine3 Weddings website change.

Any updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be amended accordingly.

9. Related Privacy Information

For information about how Nine3 Weddings collects, uses and protects personal information, please see our Privacy Policy .
