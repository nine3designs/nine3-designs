Cookie
Policy.

The simple version: no trackers, no ads, no analytics - just a beautifully quiet little website.

01

The Short Version

This website keeps things deliberately simple: it sets no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no social media tracking pixels. The only cookies you may receive are strictly necessary ones from the site's hosting and content delivery network (for example, Cloudflare security cookies that keep the site safe and fast).

02

Your Enquiry Basket

The "add to enquiry" basket on the products page does not use cookies at all. Your selection is held in the memory of the page you have open, so it stays while you browse that page and disappears when you close the tab.

03

Necessary Cookies

Like almost every website, the hosting and CDN layer may place a small number of strictly necessary cookies (such as security and load-balancing tokens). These are not used to profile you and cannot be switched off without affecting how the site works.

04

Managing Cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how this site displays or performs for you.

Because this site uses no tracking cookies, there is no advertising cookie consent banner to click through - you can browse freely.

05

Questions

Questions about this policy? Send an enquiry through the contact page. Last updated September 2026.
