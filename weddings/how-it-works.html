Before You Approve

A Fresh Pair
Of Eyes Helps.

Before your artwork goes to print, please take the time to carefully check every detail.

Once approved, your stationery will be printed exactly as shown.

Please check:

  • ☆ Spelling of all names
  • ☆ Wedding date and ceremony time
  • ☆ Venue names and addresses
  • ☆ RSVP details and deadlines
  • ☆ Contact information and website links
  • ☆ Gift list information
  • ☆ Guest names on personalised stationery
  • ☆ Table numbers and seating arrangements
Top Tip

Ask a friend or family member to review your proofs too. A fresh pair of eyes can often spot something you've missed.

Frequently Asked

A Few Things
You Might Be Wondering.

How many invitations should I order? +

You'll usually need one invitation per household rather than one per guest. I recommend ordering a few extra copies for keepsakes and last-minute additions, or rounding up to the next multiple of five.

Can I make changes after approving my artwork? +

Once your artwork has been approved and sent to print, changes are no longer possible without additional reprinting costs. Please check everything carefully before giving final approval.

Can you match my wedding colours? +

Absolutely. I'll work with you to create stationery that complements your wedding theme and colour palette as closely as possible. Exact colour matching cannot always be guaranteed, particularly between screens and print, but I'll always aim for the closest possible result.

Do you offer matching on-the-day stationery? +

Yes. I can create coordinating welcome signs, seating plans, place cards, menus, table numbers, order of service booklets, favour tags, reserved seating signs, newspapers, scratchcards and more.

Can I order extra items later? +

Where possible, yes. Additional invitations and matching stationery can usually be accommodated, subject to stock levels and production schedules. Prices may vary for smaller additional quantities.

What happens if I need my stationery quickly? +

Standard turnaround is usually around 2-4 weeks from final design approval. Priority or expedited options may be available depending on the project, production schedule and delivery requirements.
