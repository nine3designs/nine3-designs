07 / 09 Brand identity · Signage

E Squared
Coffee Corner

A bold, welcoming identity for a local coffee shop with personality at its heart.

A full branding project for E Squared Coffee Corner, a local coffee shop, covering identity design through to signage and product mockups. The identity centres on a bold, condensed serif wordmark — E SQUARED — with a coffee-bean detail worked into the O, paired with a hand-drawn coffee cup icon and an “EST. 2026” subline for a grounded, established feel despite being a new opening. A circular “COFFEE CORNER” ring wraps the mark, designed specifically to work on round signage — like the hanging shop sign shown here — as well as smaller applications such as cup stickers and packaging. The palette pairs a warm blush pink with a deep red: bold and a little playful, without losing the classic, “corner coffee shop” feel the name suggests.

09 / 09 Logo design · Automotive

Drive
The Line

A logo built around precision, control and the racing line.

The logo for Drive The Line is built around the idea of precision, control and following the ideal path. The central D is transformed into a stylised racetrack, directly connecting the name with the racing line — the most efficient, controlled route through a course. A flowing track shape creates a sense of movement and speed, while a subtle chequered detail nods to the world of advanced driving and performance. Bold, forward-leaning typography, set in Expose, gives the identity confidence and energy, reflecting the focus and quick decision-making required in Emergency Code 3 driver training. A dark neutral palette keeps the brand professional and authoritative, while a vibrant accent adds visibility and intensity. The result is a distinctive, memorable mark — modern, purposeful and rooted in driving with precision under pressure.
