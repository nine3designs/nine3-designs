Hillsborough Orthodontics

A logo concept developed for Hillsborough Orthodontics, exploring a clean, professional and approachable identity for an orthodontic practice. The design centres on a distinctive mark that balances confidence and care, the reassuring side of dentistry, with a modern, contemporary feel. Built to be versatile, the mark holds its own across digital and print, staying crisp and recognisable at every size.

The Beauty Bothy

The Beauty Bothy is a beauty brand built around a more refined, editorial take on the industry. The identity pairs elegant typography with deep navy tones and warm champagne detailing, brought together by a delicate dandelion-inspired graphic element that adds movement and lightness. The result is a brand that feels luxurious, feminine and distinctive, polished but never cold, with softness running through every touchpoint.

Tails & Tides

A fresh, coastal-inspired brand identity for Tails & Tides, built on bold typography, playful details and a calm, ocean-inspired palette. Deep teal, aqua and soft neutrals sit alongside imagery drawn from the sea, creating a brand that feels adventurous yet relaxed, made for the outdoors without losing its polish. Custom typographic details and supporting graphic elements give the identity personality, while keeping the overall look clean and contemporary.

Nordkust Coffee

The result is a distinctive coffee brand with a real sense of place, calm, considered and confident, with graphic details that carry the Nordic feel across every application, from packaging through to everyday brand moments.

Paige Oldham Hair

A print design project for Paige Oldham Hair, built around one of the hardest-working pieces a salon owns: its appointment card. The double-sided design works equally as a business card: a refined front that makes the right first impression, paired with a practical appointments reverse, so every client leaves with the brand in their pocket. Rather than starting from scratch, the existing Paige Oldham Hair logo and branding were reused and upscaled, giving the identity a sharper, more polished treatment while staying instantly recognisable. Designed and printed as an everyday salon essential, it's a small piece of design that keeps the brand present at every appointment, reminder and recommendation.

E Squared Coffee Corner

A full branding project for E Squared Coffee Corner, a local coffee shop, covering identity design through to signage and product mockups. The identity centres on a bold, condensed serif wordmark, E SQUARED, with a coffee-bean detail worked into the O, paired with a hand-drawn coffee cup icon and an “EST. 2026” subline for a grounded, established feel despite being a new opening. A circular “COFFEE CORNER” ring wraps the mark, designed specifically to work on round signage, like the hanging shop sign shown here, as well as smaller applications such as cup stickers and packaging. The palette pairs a warm blush pink with a deep red: bold and a little playful, without losing the classic, “corner coffee shop” feel the name suggests.

Drive The Line

The logo for Drive The Line is built around the idea of precision, control and following the ideal path. The central D is transformed into a stylised racetrack, directly connecting the name with the racing line, the most efficient, controlled route through a course. A flowing track shape creates a sense of movement and speed, while a subtle chequered detail nods to the world of advanced driving and performance. Bold, forward-leaning typography, set in Expose, gives the identity confidence and energy, reflecting the focus and quick decision-making required in Emergency Code 3 driver training. A dark neutral palette keeps the brand professional and authoritative, while a vibrant accent adds visibility and intensity. The result is a distinctive, memorable mark: modern, purposeful and rooted in driving with precision under pressure.

Coco Cafe

Designed for a grab and go coffee shop inspired by Caribbean flavours, health and vitality. The emblem sits inside an arched, window-like frame, a nod to the grab and go counter, with a palm frond sweeping over a halved coconut and two coffee beans on a sandy shoreline. It blends Caribbean flavour and coffee into one friendly mark that feels at home on a cup, a window or a social profile.

Lopwiel

The idea started with the moodboard: mountains easing into water became a single symbol, where the peak and the wave share one line and the water is formed in the negative space. The mountain stands for the challenge, the water for flow and recovery. Variations for different uses, from a bold app icon to a fine outline and a solid one colour mark, keep the identity consistent wherever it appears, and a small version of the symbol sits inside the o of the wordmark, tying the name and the mark together.

Duachy Apartments

The brief asked for a simple, distinctive and memorable symbol that could become recognisable as Duachy, for a brand that feels premium, established, professional and timeless. The answer is a golden eagle, the bird you see soaring over the hills, drawn as Celtic knotwork so the lines weave into each other with no start and no finish: the wildness of the Highlands woven together with Scottish heritage, and a stay that feels like it never quite ends. Some versions show the D and A in the wings as a variation. Because it is built from one continuous line, it works in any colour, and the range of colourways and type options gives the brand flexibility without ever losing recognition.

Tevuna Law

The symbol is an abstract Greek column, the classic emblem of law, simplified into clean, confident strokes. It also hides the brand's initials: the capital and central shafts form a T for Tevuna, and the two hooked outer shafts read as upside down Ls for Law. A column, a monogram and the name in one simple shape that stays clear at any size, from favicons to business cards. The wordmark is set in widely spaced capitals with the tagline beneath in a lighter treatment, so the name leads and the promise follows, and the palette of blues, from deep navy to royal and soft teal, signals trust and stability, the established language of the legal sector.

Advanced Assessment and Solutions

Sara, founder of Advanced Assessment and Solutions, came to Nine3Designs after a run of disappointing experiences with previous designers. Her existing website was a basic Canva build that no longer reflected the professionalism of her role, and she had no way to access or manage her own domain. She needed a brand and an online presence she could rely on and be proud of.

We began with the foundations. Sara was happy with her logo, so rather than redesign it, I rebuilt it as a clean, scalable version that works across print and digital. To bring the brand to life, I introduced a considered colour palette in place of the muted greys it had relied on, giving it warmth and personality while keeping it credible and professional. From there, I created a set of social media graphics to establish a consistent look across her channels. With the new identity in place, Sara trusted me to take on the biggest step: a complete website rebuild.

The new website, advancedassessmentandsolutions.co.uk, replaces the old site (advancedassessmentandsolutions.com) with a polished, professional presence that reflects Sara's expertise. It's fully under her control, consistent with her wider branding, and, after a few final refinements, looks exactly as she envisioned.
