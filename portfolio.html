01 / 04 Brand identity · Print · Launch

ESquared
Coffee

A full branding project for E Squared Coffee Corner, a local coffee shop, covering identity design through to signage and product mockups. The identity centres on a bold, condensed serif wordmark — "E SQUARED" — with a coffee-bean detail worked into the "O", paired with a hand-drawn coffee cup icon and an "EST. 2026" subline for a grounded, established feel despite being a new opening. The palette pairs a warm blush pink with a deep red, giving the brand a look that's bold and a little playful without losing the classic, "corner coffee shop" feel the name suggests. Deliverables: Logo & wordmark, colour palette, signage design, product mockups.

Developed across brand assets, print and everyday customer touchpoints, creating an identity that feels both distinctive and approachable.
